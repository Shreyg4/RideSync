RideSync Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

RideSync is an app for planning road trips with other people. This policy explains what information RideSync collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

Information we collect

InformationWhy
Account details: email address, password (stored hashed), username, first and last nameTo create your account, sign you in, and show who is on a trip
Profile photo (optional), chosen from your photo libraryTo show your picture to people on your trips
Trip content: trip names, dates, cover images, stops, addresses, notes, routes, trip progress, saved trips, and which trips you belong toTo provide the trip planning features and share trips with members you invite
Approximate and precise location (only if you grant permission)To suggest places near you when searching for stops. Your location is used on your device and sent to Mapbox with search requests. It is not saved to your account.
Crash and diagnostic data: error details, device model, OS and app version, and a short screen recording of the session in which an error occurredTo find and fix bugs. We remove sign-in tokens and similar secrets from error reports before they leave your device.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

  • Other trip members. People on a trip with you can see your username, profile photo, your name (if you choose to show it), and the trip's content.
  • Supabase stores account data, trip data, and photos.
  • Mapbox provides maps, place search, and driving directions. It receives search text, stop coordinates, and, if permitted, your location for nearby results.
  • Sentry receives crash and diagnostic data.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to stored data is restricted so that you can only read your own data and the trips you belong to.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, profile photo, saved trips, and trip memberships. Trips you own that still have other members are handed to another member so the trip continues for them; trips with no other members are deleted.

Crash reports are deleted automatically by Sentry after its retention period (up to 90 days).

If you can't access the app, you can request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

Children

RideSync is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above.
